Choke Sizing and Flow

Video lesson: Choke Sizing and Flow

Required Choke Size (Gilbert)

This calculator finds the choke size needed to reach a target oil rate. It uses the Gilbert equation, solved for the choke size.

Qo = (P × C1.89) / (435 × (GOR/1000)0.546)
C = [ Qo × 435 × (GOR/1000)0.546 / P ]1/1.89

Inputs and units

  • Wellhead pressure P in psia
  • Target oil rate Qo in bbl/d
  • Gas/oil ratio GOR in scf/bbl
  • Result C in 1/64 inch

Input checks in the calculator

  • All three inputs must be positive numbers.

Worked example

Using the default values on the page: P = 1,100 psia, Qo = 1,200 bbl/d and GOR = 787 scf/bbl.

C = [1,200 × 435 × (0.787)0.546 / 1,100]1/1.89
C = 24.32 /64 inch
The result is a theoretical estimate. The page says to verify the original reference of the correlation, its units and its operating range before field use.

Choke Flow Rate Estimation

This calculator estimates the gas rate and the oil rate through a choke. For oil you can pick one of three correlations: Gilbert, Ros or Achong.

Gas: Qg = (C/64)2.0871 × 479.088 × P / √(0.6 × (T + 460))
Gilbert: Qo = P × C1.89 / (435 × (R/1000)0.546)
Ros: Qo = P × C2.0 / (174.0 × (R/1000)0.5)
Achong: Qo = P × C1.88 / (382.2 × (R/1000)0.65)

Inputs and units

  • P: wellhead pressure in psia
  • C: manifold choke in 1/64 inch
  • R (GOR): gas/oil ratio in scf/bbl
  • T: wellhead temperature in °F
  • Results: gas rate in Mscf/d and oil rate in bbl/d

Worked example

P = 1,100 psia, C = 32/64 inch, GOR = 800 scf/bbl and T = 120 °F, using the Gilbert correlation.

Qg = (32/64)2.0871 × 479.088 × 1,100 / √(0.6 × 580) = 6,648.7 Mscf/d
Qo = 1,100 × 321.89 / (435 × 0.80.546) = 1,997.8 bbl/d
The three oil correlations use different constants, so they give different answers for the same inputs. Pick the one your field procedure calls for.

Use the Choke Flow Rate Estimation Calculator to check your own numbers.

Choke Bean Size and Liquid Flow Scaling

When the choke bean is changed at similar pressure, the new liquid rate can be estimated from the ratio of the bean areas. Since the area depends on the diameter squared, the ratio uses the squared bean sizes.

A2/A1 = (Choke Bean2 / Choke Bean1)2, with A = πd2/4
Qnew,2 = (A2/A1) × Qnew,1
ΔQ = Qnew,2 − Qnew,1

Inputs and units

  • Initial and new choke bean, in 64ths of an inch or in inches (same unit for both)
  • Initial liquid flow in STB/d (or bbl/d) or m³/d

Input checks in the calculator

  • The initial choke bean must be greater than zero.
  • The new choke bean and the initial flow must be zero or greater.

Worked example

Initial bean = 16/64, new bean = 24/64 and initial flow = 600 STB/d.

A2/A1 = (24 / 16)2 = 2.25
Qnew,2 = 2.25 × 600 = 1,350 STB/d
ΔQ = 1,350 − 600 = +750 STB/d
This simple scaling assumes similar upstream and downstream pressures and steady liquid flow. Verify the actual rates on the test separator readings.

Effective Area of Twin Chokes

When two chokes are open together, the calculator gives one combined choke size, in 64ths of an inch.

Combined = √(D12 + D22)

Inputs and units

  • D1: diameter of choke 1 in 64ths
  • D2: diameter of choke 2 in 64ths
  • Result: combined choke in 64ths

Worked example

Choke 1 = 48/64 and choke 2 = 64/64.

Combined = √(482 + 642)
Combined = √(2,304 + 4,096) = √6,400 = 80.0 /64 inch
The diameters are squared and added because flow area depends on the diameter squared.

Choke Calibration (Erosion Check)

A choke wears with time. This calculator uses the actual separator gas rate to find the true effective choke size, and compares it with the nominal choke size.

Deff = 128 × √( Qmscf/d × √(TR × γg × Z) / (π × C × Ppsia × Cd) )

Inputs and units

  • Upstream temperature in °F (TR = °F + 460)
  • Upstream pressure in psig (Ppsia = psig + 14.7)
  • Gas S.G. (γg)
  • Actual rate in mscf/d and nominal choke in 64ths
  • Constants in the calculator: C = 605.4, Cd = 0.83, Z = 0.9

Erosion and washout guide (from the page)

Variance (effective minus nominal)StatusAction
Less than 1.5/64"IntactNormal operations.
1.5 to 3.0/64"WornMonitor closely for sand production.
More than 3.0/64"Washed OutSwitch to standby choke and inspect bean.

Worked example

Actual rate = 5,800 mscf/d, T = 150 °F, P = 1,000 psig and S.G. = 0.7.

Deff = 34.1 /64 inch
Compare with the nominal choke size. If the nominal choke is 32/64, the variance is 2.1/64, which the guide calls Worn.
A choke that reads larger than nominal by more than the guide limits is washed out. Switch to the standby choke and inspect the bean.
