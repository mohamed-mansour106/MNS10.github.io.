How to Operate Diesel Tank

1- Diesel Tank Air Driven Pump (Diaphragm Pump) Operation Procedure

  • Startup Procedure:
    1. Verify that the pump is properly rigged up and connected to the required suction and discharge lines.
    2. Inspect all hoses, fittings, clamps, and connections for damage, looseness, or leakage.
    3. Ensure the compressed air supply is available and the air hose is securely connected to the pump air inlet.
    4. Confirm that the suction line is submerged in the liquid and free from restrictions.
    5. Ensure the discharge line is routed to the correct destination and all required valves are correctly lined up.
    6. Slowly open the air inlet valve to allow compressed air to enter the pump.
    7. Allow the pump to cycle at approximately 30–60 cycles per minute to establish priming and initiate liquid transfer.
    8. Observe the pump operation and verify that liquid is being discharged normally.
    9. After pumping starts, gradually adjust the air inlet valve to achieve the desired flow rate and transfer capacity.
    10. Monitor the pump for smooth cycling, stable flow, and proper performance throughout the operation.
    11. Inspect all suction and discharge connections for any signs of leakage.
    12. Monitor the air exhaust area for moisture build-up or icing under high humidity or high discharge pressure conditions.
    13. If air exhaust freezing affects pump performance, apply a suitable non-sticky anti-freeze lubricant through the air line lubricator as required.
    14. Continue monitoring liquid transfer, pump cycling rate, and overall system performance until the operation is completed.
  • Shutdown Procedure:
    1. Gradually reduce the pump operating rate by decreasing the compressed air supply if required.
    2. Close the air inlet valve to stop the compressed air supply to the pump.
    3. Verify that the pump has completely stopped cycling and liquid transfer has ceased.
    4. Inspect the pump and connected pipework for any leakage or abnormal conditions.
    5. Depressurize and isolate the air supply system if maintenance or disconnection is required.
    6. Secure the pump and associated equipment in accordance with site safety procedures.
