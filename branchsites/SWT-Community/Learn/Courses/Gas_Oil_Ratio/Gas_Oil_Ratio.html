Gas Oil Ratio: GOR, OGR and CGR

Video lesson: Gas Oil Ratio

Gas to Oil Ratio (GOR)

The Gas to Oil Ratio (GOR) is calculated from the gas and oil production rates measured during a well test.

GOR = (Gas Rate × 106) / Oil Rate

Inputs

  • Gas Rate in MMSCF/day
  • Oil Rate in STB/day

Input checks in the calculator

  • Gas Rate must not be negative.
  • Oil Rate must be greater than zero, because the oil rate is the divisor.

Worked example

Gas Rate = 2.5 MMSCF/day and Oil Rate = 1,000 STB/day.

GOR = (2.5 × 1,000,000) / 1,000
GOR = 2,500,000 / 1,000
GOR = 2,500 scf/STB
Gas in MMSCF/day multiplied by 106 gives scf/day. Dividing by STB/day gives scf/STB.

Use the Gas Oil Ratio (GOR) Calculator to check your own numbers.

Oil to Gas Ratio (OGR)

The Oil to Gas Ratio (OGR) is used to monitor the production of a volatile oil reservoir. It is the oil produced per million standard cubic feet of gas.

OGR = (Oil Rate × 106) / Gas Rate

Inputs and unit

  • Oil Rate in STB/day
  • Gas Rate in scf/day
  • Result in STB/MMSCF

Input checks in the calculator

  • Oil Rate must not be negative.
  • Gas Rate must be greater than zero, because the gas rate is the divisor.

Worked example

Oil Rate = 40 STB/day and Gas Rate = 2,000,000 scf/day.

OGR = (40 × 1,000,000) / 2,000,000
OGR = 40,000,000 / 2,000,000
OGR = 20 STB/MMSCF

Use the Oil Gas Ratio (OGR) Calculator to check your own numbers.

Condensate to Gas Ratio (CGR) and Gas to Condensate Ratio (GCR)

The CGR describes the liquid yield, or condensate content, of a gas-condensate reservoir. It is expressed in STB/MMSCF.

CGR = Condensate Rate (STB/d) / Gas Rate (MMSCFD)
GCR = 1,000,000 / CGR

The GCR is the equivalent Gas to Condensate Ratio, in scf/STB.

Inputs

  • Condensate Rate in STB/d
  • Gas Rate in MMSCFD (million scf/d) or in SCFD (standard cubic feet per day)
Field note: when the gas rate is in MMSCFD, dividing the condensate rate (STB/d) by it gives STB/MMSCF directly. If you enter the gas rate in SCFD, switch the unit selector to SCFD. The calculator then converts it with MMSCFD = Gas Rate (SCFD) / 106.

Worked example

Condensate Rate = 150 STB/d and Gas Rate = 5 MMSCFD.

CGR = 150 / 5 = 30 STB/MMSCF
GCR = 1,000,000 / 30 = 33,333 scf/STB

Solution GOR (Rs) Estimation

The solution GOR estimates how much gas is dissolved in the oil. The Solution GOR calculator uses two correlations: Standing (1947) and Vasquez & Beggs. Both give Rs in scf/stb.

Inputs

  • Separator Pressure in psig (the calculator adds 14.7 to get psia)
  • Oil Temperature in °F
  • Gas Gravity
  • Oil API

Standing (1947)

Rs = γg × [ P / (18 × 10yg) ]1.204
yg = 0.00091 × T − 0.0125 × API

Vasquez & Beggs

Rs = C1 × γg × PC2 × exp( C3 × API / Tabs )
Tabs = T + 460

Bubble Point for Oil

The Bubble Point calculator estimates the bubble point pressure (Pb) with the Vasquez-Beggs correlation. It first corrects the gas gravity to separator conditions.

Inputs

  • Oil Gravity in °API
  • GOR in scf/bbl
  • Gas Gravity (Air = 1)
  • Separator Temperature in °F
  • Separator Pressure in psig
  • Reservoir Temperature in °F

Step 1: corrected gas gravity

γgc = γg × [ 1 + 0.00005912 × API × Tsep × log10( Psep / 114.7 ) ]

Step 2: bubble point pressure

Pb = [ Rs / ( C1 × γgc × exp( C3 × API / (Tres + 460) ) ) ]1 / C2
