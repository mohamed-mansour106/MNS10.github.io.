General Well Testing Calculations: Gas and Choke

This page groups many quick field calculators. The first group covers gas velocity, gas volume and the twin choke equivalent size.

Gas velocity: V = 2122 × Bg × Qmscf / d², with Bg = 0.02833 × Z × T / P (T in °R)
Gas volume (velocity based): Q = V × d² / (2122 × Bg)
Gas volume (choke / pressure based): Q = Ppsia × (d64 / 64)² × 24
Equivalent twin choke: Deq = √(d1² + d2²)

Inputs and units

  • Z factor (the field is labelled Z Factor (1/Fpv)), pressure in psia, temperature in °F, line ID in inches
  • Gas Q in mscf/d (for velocity) or velocity in ft/s (for volume)
  • Choke sizes in 64ths (twin choke: choke 1 and choke 2), results as 64ths and inches

Worked example

Gas: Z = 0.9, 1,000 psia, 100°F, 4 in line and 50 mscf/d, calculated the way the page code does it (with 1/Z). Choke: 1,000 psia and a 32/64 choke. Twin choke: 16/64 and 24/64.

Velocity = 116.89 ft/s
Q = 1,000 × (32/64)² × 24 = 6,000
Deq = √(16² + 24²) = 28.8 /64 in = 0.451 in
The printed Bg formula uses Z, while the page code uses 1/Z in the velocity and volume. Check which Z your field sheet gives before you enter it.

General Well Testing Calculations: Oil, Gravity and Shrinkage

The second group of the same page covers the Gilbert oil rate, the gravity correction to 60°F, the API and S.G. conversions and the shrinkage factor.

Oil: Qoil = P × D1.89 / [435 × (GOR/1000)0.546]; water = oil × BSW/100; gas = oil × GOR / 1,000,000 (MMSCFD)
SG60 = SGobs + [0.00069 − 0.000372 × SGobs] × (Tobs − 60)
API = 141.5 / SG − 131.5, and SG = 141.5 / (API + 131.5)
Shrinkage = 1 / Bo, Bo = 0.9759 + 0.00012 × F1.2

Inputs and units

  • Oil: pressure in psig, GOR in scf/bbl, choke diameter in 64ths, BSW in %
  • Gravity: observed S.G. and observed temperature in °F
  • Shrinkage: S.G. at 60°F, oil temperature in °F, separator pressure in psig

Worked example

Oil: 500 psig, 24/64, GOR 800 and BSW 10%. Gravity: S.G. 0.80 at 80°F. Shrinkage: S.G. 0.80, 90°F and 100 psig.

Oil = 527.2 bopd, water = 52.7 bpd, gas = 0.4218 MMSCFD
SG60 = 0.8078 and API60 = 43.66
F = 161.47, Bo = 1.0295, shrinkage = 0.971
The page water rate is the oil rate times BSW/100, a quick estimate. The shrinkage model is the same one the Shrinkage course covers.

Advanced Well Test Calculator

This calculator takes the oil meter readings and the gas orifice meter data of a test and gives the oil rate, gas rate, GOR and OGR together.

Vnet = (Metercurr − Meterprev) × MF × Sh × (1 − BSW)
Qo = Vnet × (1440 / Interval) × Ctl, where Ctl is the volume correction factor (K-factor) from the API at 60°F and the oil line temperature
Gas rate: AGA-3 orifice method from meter run size, orifice size, pressure, differential pressure, gravity and temperature
GOR = Qg × 1000 / Qo, OGR = Qo / (Qg / 1000)

Inputs and units

  • Oil: interval in min, observed API, hydrometer temperature, current and previous meter in bbl, oil line temperature, BS&W %, meter factor, shrinkage factor
  • Gas: meter run size and orifice plate in inches, separator pressure in psig, differential pressure in inH₂O, gas gravity, gas line temperature
  • Results: oil rate in bpd, gas rate in mscfd, GOR in scf/bbl and OGR in bbl/mmscf

Worked example

Interval 60 min, observed API 40, hydrometer 80°F, meter 1,000 to 1,500 bbl, oil line 85°F, BS&W 5%, MF 1.01 and shrinkage 0.97. Gas: 4 in run, 2 in plate, 500 psig, 100 inH₂O, gravity 0.65 and 90°F (example readings chosen for this course).

API at 60°F = 38.32 and K-factor = 0.98799
Vnet = 500 × 1.01 × 0.97 × 0.95 = 465.36 bbl
Qo = 465.36 × 24 × 0.98799 = 11,034.5 bpd
Gas rate = 5,767 mscfd, GOR = 523 scf/bbl, OGR = 1,913.46 bbl/mmscf
Hydrometer and line temperatures must be accurate, because the volume correction is very sensitive to temperature. The page also tells you to check the GOR to make sure the well flows above bubble point.

DPU Chart Reading Calculator

Use this when the chart range differs from the full-scale range of the differential pressure unit (DPU). It converts the actual DPU reading to the chart scale.

Chart Reading = Actual Reading × (Chart Range / DPU Range)
% DPU span = Actual Reading / DPU Range × 100
% Chart span = Chart Reading / Chart Range × 100

Inputs and units

  • Actual reading, for example in inH₂O or psi
  • Chart range (full scale of the chart)
  • DPU range (full scale of the DPU, it must be above zero)
  • Keep all three in matching units

Worked example

Actual reading 60, chart range 100 and DPU range 150.

Chart reading = 60 × (100 / 150) = 40.00
% DPU span = 60 / 150 × 100 = 40.00%
% Chart span = 40.00 / 100 × 100 = 40.00%
When the chart range equals the DPU range, the chart reading equals the actual reading.
