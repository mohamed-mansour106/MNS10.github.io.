BS&W / W.C. (Water Cut) Calculator

Calculate water cut (\(\text{BS\&W}\) or \(\text{WC}\)) from water rate and total liquid rate (\(Q_{\text{liquid}}\)) or oil rate (\(Q_{\text{oil}}\)).

Water Cut Percentage (BS&W %)

$$\text{BS\&W (\%)} = \frac{Q_{\text{water}}}{Q_{\text{liquid}}} \times 100 = \frac{Q_{\text{water}}}{Q_{\text{water}} + Q_{\text{oil}}} \times 100$$

Water Cut Fraction (Decimal)

$$\text{WC} = \frac{Q_{\text{water}}}{Q_{\text{liquid}}} = \frac{Q_{\text{water}}}{Q_{\text{water}} + Q_{\text{oil}}}$$

Derived Stream Summary

$$Q_{\text{liquid}} = Q_{\text{water}} + Q_{\text{oil}}$$

Field Note: Ensure consistent units across both flow rates (both in \(\text{STB/d}\) or both in \(\text{m}^3/\text{d}\)). In well testing, BS&W (Basic Sediment & Water) and WC (Water Cut) represent the water share of total liquid production.
