10. Condensate to Gas Ratio (CGR)

Gas-condensate reservoir liquid yield / condensate content expressed in STB/MMSCF.

Condensate-to-Gas Ratio (CGR)

$$\text{CGR} = \frac{\text{Condensate Rate (STB/d)}}{\text{Gas Rate (MMSCFD)}}$$

Equivalent Gas-to-Condensate (GCR)

$$\text{GCR} = \frac{1{,}000{,}000}{\text{CGR}}$$

Field Note: When gas rate is measured in MMSCFD, dividing condensate rate (STB/d) by gas rate (MMSCFD) directly gives STB/MMSCF. If you enter gas rate in SCFD, switch the unit selector to SCFD so the calculator converts it using \(\text{MMSCFD} = \text{Gas Rate}_{\text{SCFD}} / 10^6\).
