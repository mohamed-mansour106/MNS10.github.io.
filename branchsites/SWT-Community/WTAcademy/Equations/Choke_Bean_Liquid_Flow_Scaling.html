Choke Bean Area & Liquid Flow Scaling

Estimate new liquid flow rate (\(Q_{\text{new},2}\)) when changing choke bean size at similar pressure using the area ratio \(\frac{A_2}{A_1} = \left(\frac{\text{Choke Bean}_2}{\text{Choke Bean}_1}\right)^2\).

Uses the same unit selected on the left

Estimated Liquid Flow (\(Q_{\text{new},2}\))

$$Q_{\text{new},2} = \frac{A_2}{A_1} \times Q_{\text{new},1}$$

Area Ratio (\(A_2 / A_1\))

$$\frac{A_2}{A_1} = \left(\frac{\text{Choke Bean}_2}{\text{Choke Bean}_1}\right)^2, \quad A = \frac{\pi d^2}{4}$$

Flow Change (\(\Delta Q\))

$$\Delta Q = Q_{\text{new},2} - Q_{\text{new},1}$$

Field Note: Because choke orifice area scales with diameter squared (\(A = \frac{\pi d^2}{4}\), so \(A \propto d^2\)), increasing choke bean size increases flow area rapidly. In the field, this simple area-ratio scaling assumes comparable upstream/downstream pressures and single-phase/steady liquid behavior; verify actual rates on test separator readings.
