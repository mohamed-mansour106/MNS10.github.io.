DPU Chart Reading Calculator

Convert a Differential Pressure Unit (DPU) actual reading to chart scale when the chart range differs from the DPU full-scale range.

Measured value (e.g. in. H&sub2;O or psi)
Full scale of the chart
Full scale of the DPU (≠ 0)

Chart Reading

$$\text{Chart Reading} = \text{Actual Reading} \times \frac{\text{Chart Range}}{\text{DPU Range}}$$

% of DPU Range

$$\%\text{ DPU Span} = \frac{\text{Actual Reading}}{\text{DPU Range}} \times 100$$

% of Chart Range

$$\%\text{ Chart Span} = \frac{\text{Chart Reading}}{\text{Chart Range}} \times 100$$

Field Note: Keep Actual Reading, Chart Range, and DPU Range in matching units (for example, all in inches of water or all in psig). When the Chart Range equals the DPU Range, the Chart Reading equals the Actual Reading.
