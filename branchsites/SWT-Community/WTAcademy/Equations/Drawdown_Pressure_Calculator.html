Drawdown Pressure Equation (\(P_r - P_{wf}\))

Calculate reservoir pressure drawdown from Reservoir Pressure (\(P_r\)) and Bottomhole Flowing Pressure (\(P_{wf}\)), with optional Wellhead Pressure (\(P_{wh}\)) context.

Uses the pressure unit selected on the left
Optional surface flowing pressure

Drawdown (\(\Delta P_{\text{dd}}\))

$$\text{Drawdown} = P_r - P_{wf}$$

Total Drop (\(P_r - P_{wh}\))

$$\Delta P_{\text{total}} = P_r - P_{wh}$$

String / Tubing Drop (\(P_{wf} - P_{wh}\))

$$\Delta P_{\text{string}} = P_{wf} - P_{wh}$$

Field Note: Keep \(P_r\), \(P_{wf}\), and \(P_{wh}\) on a consistent pressure basis (all absolute or all gauge in the same unit). If \(P_{wf}\) is higher than \(P_r\), the result is negative (indicating injection or pressure buildup above flowing level rather than production drawdown).
