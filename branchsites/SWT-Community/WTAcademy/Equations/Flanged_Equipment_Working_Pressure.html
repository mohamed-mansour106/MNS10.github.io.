15. Working Pressure for Flanged Equipment (e.g., Separator)

Quick field estimate of approximate Maximum Allowable Working Pressure (MAWP) for Low Pressure (LP) and Medium Pressure (MP) ANSI/ASA flange classes.

Class ≤ 150 uses 1.9 (LP); Class > 150 uses 2.4 (MP)

Estimated Working Pressure (psig)

$$\text{WP}_{\text{LP}} = \text{Class} \times 1.9, \quad \text{WP}_{\text{MP}} = \text{Class} \times 2.4$$

Working Pressure (bar)

$$1\text{ bar} \approx 14.5038\text{ psi}$$

Working Pressure (kPa) & Factor

$$1\text{ bar} = 100\text{ kPa}$$

Field Note: These multipliers (1.9× for Low Pressure and 2.4× for Medium Pressure classes 300, 600, and 900) are rapid well-testing field rules of thumb. Actual pressure-temperature ratings depend on flange material group (e.g., carbon steel A105), gasket type, and operating temperature per ASME B16.5 / B16.34 and vessel nameplates.
