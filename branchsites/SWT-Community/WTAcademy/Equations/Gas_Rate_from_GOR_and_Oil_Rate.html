Gas Rate (MMSCFD) from GOR & Oil Rate

Calculate gas flow rate in million standard cubic feet per day (\(\text{MMSCFD}\)) from oil rate (\(\text{STBPD}\) / \(\text{STB/d}\)) and Gas-Oil Ratio (\(\text{GOR}\)).

Units: STBPD (STB/d)
Units: scf/STB

Gas Rate (MMSCFD)

$$Q_{\text{gas, MMSCFD}} = \frac{Q_{\text{Oil, STBPD}} \times \text{GOR}}{10^6}$$

Gas Rate (MSCFD)

$$Q_{\text{gas, MSCFD}} = Q_{\text{gas, MMSCFD}} \times 1{,}000$$

Total Gas Rate (SCFD)

$$Q_{\text{gas, SCFD}} = Q_{\text{Oil, STBPD}} \times \text{GOR}$$

Field Note: Dividing \(Q_{\text{Oil, STBPD}} \times \text{GOR}\) by \(10^6\) (\(1{,}000{,}000\)) converts standard cubic feet per day (\(\text{SCFD}\)) directly into million standard cubic feet per day (\(\text{MMSCFD}\)).
