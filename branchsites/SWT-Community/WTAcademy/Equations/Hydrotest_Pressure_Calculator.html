Hydrotest Pressure Calculator

Calculate required hydrostatic test pressure using the standard 1.5× Working Pressure rule of thumb across field pressure units.

Standard field factor: 1.5

Hydrotest Pressure (psig)

$$P_{\text{test}} = P_{\text{working}} \times 1.5$$

Hydrotest Pressure (bar)

$$1\text{ bar} \approx 14.5038\text{ psi}$$

Hydrotest Pressure (kPa)

$$1\text{ bar} = 100\text{ kPa}$$

Field Note: Always verify equipment Maximum Allowable Working Pressure (MAWP), lowest-rated downstream component limits, temperature derating, and site HSE permits before pressurizing any line or skid.
