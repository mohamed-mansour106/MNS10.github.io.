Net Oil Rate from Gross & Water Rate

Calculate net oil production rate by subtracting water rate from gross liquid rate.

Use matching rate unit (STB/d or m³/d)

Net Oil Rate (\(Q_{\text{oil}}\))

$$\text{Net Oil Rate} = \text{Gross} - \text{Water Rate}$$

Water Cut (\(\text{WC} \%\))

$$\text{WC (\%)} = \left(\frac{\text{Water Rate}}{\text{Gross}}\right) \times 100$$

Oil Cut Share

$$\text{Oil Cut (\%)} = 100 - \text{WC (\%)}$$

Field Note: Keep Gross Liquid Rate and Water Rate in the same unit. For example, a gross liquid rate of 1,500 STB/d and water rate of 300 STB/d yield a net oil rate of 1,200.00 STB/d (20.00% WC).
