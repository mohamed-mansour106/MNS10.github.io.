Oil Flow Estimates – WHP, GOR & Choke Size (\(D\))

Quick field estimate of oil flow rate (\(Q_{\text{oil}}\)) from Wellhead Pressure (\(\text{WHP}\)), Gas-Oil Ratio (\(\text{GOR}\)), and choke size (\(D\)).

Units: scf/STB

Estimated Oil Rate (STB/d)

$$Q_{\text{oil}} = \frac{\text{WHP} \times D^{1.89}}{435} \times \left(\frac{\text{GOR}}{1}\right)^{0.546}$$

Estimated Oil Rate (m³/d)

$$1\text{ STB} \approx 0.158987\text{ m}^3$$

Associated Gas Rate (MSCFD)

$$Q_{\text{gas}} = \frac{Q_{\text{oil}} \times \text{GOR}}{1{,}000}$$

Field Note: In oilfield choke correlations, \(D\) is standardly entered in 64ths of an inch (e.g., 32 for 32/64" = 1/2"); if you select Inches (in), the calculator converts \(D_{64} = D_{\text{in}} \times 64\). Treat empirical choke estimates as preliminary screening values and verify against separator meter rates.
