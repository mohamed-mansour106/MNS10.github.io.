Oil Rate Calculation

Calculate corrected oil production rate using BS&W or oil shrinkage correction methods.

Method 1: BS&W Correction
$$Q_{Oil}=KF \times MF \times INC \times Int.T$$
$$\times\left(1-\frac{BS\&W}{100}\right)$$
Method 2: Oil Shrinkage Correction
$$Q_{Liquid}=Int.T \times MK$$
$$\times OilShrinkage \times MeterInt_{Oil} \times KF$$

Calculation Notes
  • KF: Volume Correction Factor.
  • MF: Meter Factor.
  • INC: Increment multiplier.
  • Int.T: Meter total increment.
  • BS&W: Basic Sediment and Water percentage.
  • MK: Meter constant or multiplier.
  • Oil Shrinkage: Shrinkage correction factor.
  • Meter Int Oil: Oil meter increment.
