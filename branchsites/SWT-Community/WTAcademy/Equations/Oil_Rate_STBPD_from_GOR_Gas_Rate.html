Oil Rate (STBPD) from GOR & Gas Rate

Calculate oil production rate (\(\text{STBPD}\)) from gas flow rate (\(\text{MMSCFD}\)) and Gas-Oil Ratio (\(\text{GOR}\)).

Units: MMSCFD (million SCFD)
Units: scf/STB (> 0)

Oil Rate (STBPD)

$$Q_{\text{Oil, STBPD}} = \frac{Q_{\text{gas, MMSCFD}} \times 10^6}{\text{GOR}}$$

Oil Rate (m³/d)

$$1\text{ STB} \approx 0.158987\text{ m}^3$$

Total Gas Volume (SCFD)

$$Q_{\text{gas, SCFD}} = Q_{\text{gas, MMSCFD}} \times 10^6$$

Field Note: Multiplying gas rate in MMSCFD by \(10^6\) (\(1{,}000{,}000\)) gives total gas flow in SCFD, and dividing by GOR (\(\text{scf/STB}\)) returns the estimated oil rate in STBPD.
