Pressure Law Equation (\(P = F / A\))

Calculate mechanical or fluid pressure (\(P\)) from applied normal force (\(F\)) acting over a cross-sectional area (\(A\)).

Pressure (psi)

$$P = \frac{F}{A} \quad (\text{lbf/in}^2)$$

Pressure (bar)

$$1\text{ bar} \approx 14.5038\text{ psi}$$

Pressure (kPa)

$$1\text{ Pa} = 1\text{ N/m}^2, \quad 1\text{ kPa} = 1000\text{ Pa}$$

Field Note: Force and area are automatically converted to consistent units before dividing. For a circular cross-section such as a piston, seal, or pipe bore of diameter \(d\), \(A = \frac{\pi}{4} d^2\).
