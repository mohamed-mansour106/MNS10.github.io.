Specific Gravity Equation Calculator

Calculate oil Specific Gravity (\(\text{SG}\) at \(60^\circ\text{F}/60^\circ\text{F}\)) from API Gravity (°API) along with reference density equivalents.

Requires \(\text{API Gravity} + 131.5 > 0\)
Based on standard API gravity bands

Specific Gravity (\(\text{SG}\))

$$\text{SG} = \frac{141.5}{\text{API Gravity} + 131.5}$$

Density at 60°F (kg/m³)

$$\rho \approx \text{SG} \times 999.016\text{ kg/m}^3$$

Density at 60°F (lb/ft³)

$$\rho \approx \text{SG} \times 62.366\text{ lb/ft}^3$$

Field Note: Light crude oils typically have higher °API (e.g., 35–45 °API) and lower specific gravity (~0.80–0.85), whereas heavier oils have lower °API and specific gravity closer to or above 1.00.
