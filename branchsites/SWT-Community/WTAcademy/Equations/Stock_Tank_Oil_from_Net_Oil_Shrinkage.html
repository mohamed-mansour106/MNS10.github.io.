Stock Tank Oil (\(\text{STO}\)) from Net Oil & Shrinkage@60°F

Calculate Stock Tank Oil (\(\text{STO}\)) by applying the shrinkage factor at 60°F to the metered or calculated Net Oil rate.

Stock Tank Oil (\(\text{STO}\))

$$\text{STO} = \text{Net Oil} \times \text{Shrinkage}@60^\circ\text{F}$$

Shrinkage Volume Loss

$$\text{Loss} = \text{Net Oil} - \text{STO}$$

Volume Factor (\(1 / \text{Shrinkage}\))

$$\text{Factor} = \frac{1}{\text{Shrinkage}@60^\circ\text{F}}$$

Field Note: When shrinkage at 60°F is given as a decimal factor (such as 0.92), multiply directly by Net Oil. If your lab or PVT sheet provides a percentage (such as 92%), switch the format selector to Percentage (%) so it divides by 100 automatically.
