True Produced Gas (Gas Lift Subtraction)

Isolate reservoir-produced gas by subtracting injected gas-lift rate from the total metered/measured gas rate at surface.

Uses the same gas rate unit selected on the left
STB/d (for True GOR check when unit is SCFD/MSCFD/MMSCFD)

True Produced Gas

$$\text{True Produced Gas} = \text{Measured} - \text{Lift Gas}$$

Lift Gas Share of Measured

$$\text{Lift Share (\%)} = \left(\frac{\text{Lift Gas}}{\text{Measured}}\right) \times 100$$

True Producing GOR

$$\text{GOR}_{\text{true}} = \frac{\text{True Gas (scfd)}}{Q_{\text{oil}}\text{ (STB/d)}}$$

Field Note: Ensure both Measured Gas Rate and Gas Injection Lift are normalized to the same base conditions and units before subtracting. If injected lift gas exceeds the measured total gas rate, check lift metering calibration or transient well unloading effects.
