Water Rate from Gross & Water Cut (\(\text{WC}\))

Calculate water production rate (\(Q_W\)) from gross liquid rate and percentage water cut (\(\text{WC}\)).

Percentage from 0% to 100%

Water Rate (\(Q_W\))

$$Q_W = \text{Gross} \times \left(\frac{\text{WC}}{100}\right)$$

Net Oil Rate (\(Q_{\text{oil}}\))

$$Q_{\text{oil}} = \text{Gross} - Q_W$$

Oil Cut Share

$$\text{Oil Cut (\%)} = 100 - \text{WC}$$

Field Note: Divide the percentage water cut by 100 before multiplying by the gross liquid rate. For example, a gross rate of 1,000 STB/d at 25% WC gives Q_W = 250.00 STB/d and net oil rate 750.00 STB/d.
