Talk English

Practical English for oilfield and well testing engineers. Short phrases you can use on shift, in reports and in meetings.

1. Daily report and shift handover

  • "The well was flowing at a stable rate of 1,200 bbl/d at the end of my shift."
  • "We shut in at 14:30 and recorded the pressure build-up for six hours."
  • "Nothing to report on safety. No incidents, no near misses."
  • "Please check the choke size and the separator pressure when you take over."

2. Describing a well test

  • "We ran a drawdown test followed by a build-up."
  • "The pressure derivative shows radial flow after wellbore storage ends."
  • "The data quality is good, but the gauge drifted during the last hour."
  • "The rate was not constant, so we need to correct for rate changes."

3. Safety and stopping the job

  • "I am stopping the job. The pressure is above the approved limit."
  • "Is this equipment rated for this working pressure?"
  • "Can we do a toolbox talk before we start?"
  • "Everyone clear of the high-pressure line, please."

4. Radio and phone calls

  • "Control, this is the test unit. Do you copy?" Reply: "Copy" means I understood.
  • "Say again" means please repeat. "Stand by" means wait.
  • "Over" means I finished speaking. "Out" means the conversation is finished.
  • Spell names and numbers clearly: "Well A-3, one-two-zero-zero barrels per day".

5. Meetings and emails

  • "Could you clarify what you mean by stabilised flow?"
  • "My recommendation is to extend the build-up by four hours."
  • "Please find attached the test report. Let me know if you have any questions."
  • "I will send you the updated data by the end of the day."

A simple practice routine

  • Write your daily report in English, even if you also write it in Arabic.
  • Read one page of a well testing book aloud each day.
  • Record yourself explaining one test result in 60 seconds and listen back.
  • Learn the terms in the dictionary and terms and abbreviations pages.
