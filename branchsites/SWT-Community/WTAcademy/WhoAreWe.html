What is OFU?

What you will find on OFU.

A growing collection of learning resources, engineering references and community-driven knowledge for people entering or working in oil and gas.

🎓

Structured Learning

Step-by-step resources covering oilfield fundamentals, well testing concepts, equipment and operational practices.

🛠️

Equipment & Operations

Explore separators, choke manifolds, burners and booms, heaters, pumps, strainers and safety systems.

📈

Engineering Tools

Practical calculators, equations and references for field calculations and pressure transient concepts.

💻

DAQ & Digital Oilfield

Learn about real-time data acquisition, field measurements, software concepts and digital workflows.

🧊

3D Models & Simulation

Visual learning experiences that help explain equipment layouts, components and operating principles.

🤝

Community & Knowledge Sharing

Ask technical questions, share field insights and learn through discussions with other oilfield learners.

The Founder

Built from real field experience.

OFU was founded by Mohamed Mansour (MMM106), a Well Testing professional whose field experience inspired a practical, accessible approach to technical learning.

MMMohamed Mansour
Founder of OilField University

Working in Well Testing and Data Acquisition, Mohamed has worked in surface well testing since 2022. His professional interests include field operations, real-time monitoring, DAQ systems, Fekete workflows, Modbus communication, operational reporting and digital tools for technical learning.

OFU is his initiative to organize practical knowledge, explain engineering concepts in a learner-friendly way and help bridge the gap between classroom fundamentals and field applications.
