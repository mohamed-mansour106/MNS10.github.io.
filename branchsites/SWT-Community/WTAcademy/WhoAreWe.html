Who Are We

OilField University and the Well Testing Academy: a free place to learn oil and gas field skills.

What this is

OilField University (OFU) and the Well Testing Academy make up a free learning platform for people who work in oil and gas, with a focus on well testing. You will find certification guides, structured courses, equations, a dictionary, short field notes on the blog, 3D models of field equipment, and a community where you can ask questions and share what you think.

Who builds it

The platform is an independent project by Mohamed Mansour (MMM106), a well testing field operator.

Why it is free

Field knowledge should be easy to reach. Everything here is open to read, and you can sign in to the community to ask questions, add structured courses and share posts.
